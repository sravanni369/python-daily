========================================================================
4. THE FAIR READING
========================================================================
Geron removes these rows on purpose. His notebook stores them as
`missing_data` so he can plot them, and Chapter 1 comes back to them as the
worked example of sampling bias. The filtering is a teaching setup.

What it costs is this: Example 1-1 is the first runnable code in the book and
the piece most people copy. On its own it shows a relationship about twice as
strong as the full data supports, and the printed listing gives no sign that
seven countries were removed to get there.
